Voccab bundled Simple English dictionary

54,055 word forms and 73,090 senses, from Simple English Wiktionary contributors via Kaikki/Wiktextract. Downloaded for the 7 October 2026 release. The raw file checksum and generated counts are recorded in simple-v1/manifest.json.

The bundled adapted dictionary data is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This applies to the dictionary data, not automatically to the app code. Original word pages credit contributing authors through their page histories.

Changes: retain English entries with a usable definition and headwords at most 120 characters; remove unused fields; group by headword and prefix; order explicitly obsolete/offensive senses after other senses; preserve each definition with its own example and usage labels. Case variants remain separate records. Definition and example wording has not been rewritten. Links to base forms are shown separately. No audio is bundled.

This is a community-maintained dataset and may contain errors, old senses, specialist language or sensitive vocabulary. Word forms include inflections and variants, not only everyday headwords. It is not a complete English dictionary.

Broader online word suggestions: https://www.datamuse.com/api/
Additional online definitions: https://dictionaryapi.dev/
External definitions and recordings retain their own supplied licences.

To rebuild from a downloaded raw file: node scripts/import-simple-dictionary.mjs /path/to/simple-extract.jsonl.gz (from src/frontend). Review updated counts and licences before publishing a new dataset.

Counts include variants, inflections and phrases, not only basic vocabulary.

Native definitions, grammatical labels, IPA and available short contributor
examples are retained. External quotations with references are omitted; see
the original pages for those examples. Explicitly obsolete/offensive senses
are ordered last. Source-supplied additional forms have a labelled base word;
no forms or meanings are invented. Headwords/forms are limited to 100 Unicode
characters. Fields are compacted, indexed and gzip-compressed into sections.
Definitions are not rewritten, automatically simplified or translated.
